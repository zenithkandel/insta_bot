Quick Start

  1. Get your credentials: Open instagram.com in your browser, open DevTools (F12), go to Application tab, then Cookies, then https://www.instagram.com. Copy the sessionid and csrftoken values.
  2. Paste them: Paste both values into the Session card above and click Save.
  3. Add recipients: Paste Instagram usernames (one per line) into the Recipients card, or import a .txt/.csv file.
  4. Write your message: Use {{username}} to auto-insert each recipient's handle.
  5. Configure pacing: Set interval (min 5s), max volume, parallel workers, and retry count.
  6. Start Campaign: Click Start and watch the live activity feed.

How It Works

This tool uses a headless Chrome browser (via Selenium) to send Instagram DMs. Unlike traditional API approaches that get blocked by Instagram's TLS fingerprinting and CORS protections, this method launches a real browser instance, injects your session cookie, and executes fetch() calls from within the browser context — making the requests indistinguishable from a real user sitting at their computer.

The flow: Resolve username to numeric ID, find existing DM thread, send via GraphQL mutation (IGDirectTextSendMutation, doc_id 28737274835943013), all from the browser's own context.

Message Variables

{{username}} Replaced with the recipient's username (no @)
{{handle}} Replaced with @ + username (e.g. @alex_designer)

Example: "Hi @{{username}}, loved your work!" becomes "Hi @alex_designer, loved your work!" when sending to alex_designer.

Scheduling & Pacing

Instagram rate-limits DMs. This tool enforces deterministic pacing to stay within limits:

Interval (sec) Minimum seconds between sends. Default: 30. Minimum: 5.
Max Volume Maximum DMs per campaign. Default: 50.
Parallel Workers Simultaneous sends. Default: 3. Range: 1-10. Higher = faster but more aggressive.
Retry Attempts Auto-retry failed sends. Default: 2. Range: 0-5.
Start slow. For a new account, use interval 60s, max volume 20, and parallel 1. Instagram may flag accounts that send too many DMs too quickly.

Limitations & Notes

Works for users who follow you back or have an existing DM thread with you.
Cold DMs to strangers may land in "Message Requests" and require approval from the recipient.
Instagram may rate-limit or temporarily block accounts that send too many DMs.
Session ID is stored locally in your browser. Never sent to any external server.
Requires Google Chrome installed on your machine (headless mode).
CSRF token is auto-extracted from Instagram cookies if not provided manually.

Troubleshooting

Why is my session not working? Make sure sessionid and csrftoken are from the SAME browser session. Open instagram.com in your browser, then copy both cookies from DevTools. If you logged out and back in, the old sessionid is invalid.
Why do I get "Message Request" errors? Instagram requires recipients to approve messages from people they don't follow. Try sending to someone who follows you back or has an existing conversation with you.
Can I send to multiple people at once? Yes. Set Parallel Workers > 1 to send to multiple recipients simultaneously. Keep it at 3 or below to avoid Instagram rate limits.
How do I know if a DM was actually sent? Check the Live Activity feed in the UI and the terminal output. Green = sent, red = failed. Each log entry shows the recipient, status, and details.
Does this work with Instagram Business accounts? Yes, as long as you use the sessionid from a Business/Creator account. The DM mechanism is the same.
